# Art direction

A human-crafted direction is a chain of decisions specific to the subject, not simulated imperfection.

## Brief

```text
Subject and offer:
Audience and context:
Communication job:
Exact message and action:
Brand assets and constraints:
Channel, size, and viewing distance:
Cultural or material references:
Anti-references:
One signature:
What must stay quiet:
```

## Reference decomposition

For each reference, record what is useful: hierarchy, crop, rhythm, typographic contrast, material, color behavior, image treatment, or tone. Never copy a finished composition or imitate a living artist's signature style.

## Human fingerprint gate

Reject a direction when:

- it could serve an unrelated brand after changing the logo;
- it starts from a trend, preset, palette catalog, or generic mood word;
- several memorable effects compete;
- fake grain, distortion, collage, handwriting, or irregularity was added only to simulate humanity;
- the rationale was invented after the visual was produced;
- it repeats a recent direction without a brief-based reason.

Pass when the concept, hierarchy, signature, restraint, and material choices trace back to the communication job and brand evidence.
