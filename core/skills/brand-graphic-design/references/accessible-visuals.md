# Accessible visuals

Apply [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) and the [WAI Images Tutorial](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/) according to the destination.

- Do not use color as the only carrier of meaning; add labels, shape, pattern, position, or direct annotation.
- Verify text and essential graphical-object contrast at final size and against the actual background.
- Keep essential text live and editable when the medium allows it. Avoid images of text except logos or cases where the presentation is essential.
- Decorative images need no descriptive alternative; informative and functional images need an alternative matching their purpose.
- Complex diagrams and infographics need a concise summary plus a complete equivalent for the conveyed information.
- Direct-label charts where practical; ensure patterns, ordering, or annotations survive grayscale and common color-vision differences.
- Test mobile crop, zoom, small-size legibility, localization expansion, and low-quality display conditions when applicable.
- Do not place essential information only in generated image text; generated lettering must be proofread against the exact approved copy.
